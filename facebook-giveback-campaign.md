# Tech Guardian Gives Back - Facebook Campaign

## THE CONCEPT
Tech Guardian offers FREE repairs to people in need - nominated by the community. Customers nominate someone struggling (single parent, veteran, student, elderly on fixed income, etc.) and Tech Guardian picks one person per month to help for free.

---

## RULES (keep simple)

- One winner per month
- Must be in service area (KC metro / Lee's Summit area)
- Device must be repairable (we'll assess)
- Nominator doesn't need to be the recipient
- No purchase necessary
- We reserve right to choose based on need and story

---

## WHY THIS WORKS

1. **Engagement** - People LOVE nominating others. Comments and shares go through the roof.
2. **Emotional** - These stories are real and hit hard. People connect.
3. **PR Gold** - Local news eats this up. "Local repair shop fixes devices free for struggling families"
4. **Community Building** - Positions Tech Guardian as the shop that CARES, not just fixes.
5. **Referrals** - Everyone who nominates someone is now thinking about Tech Guardian.
