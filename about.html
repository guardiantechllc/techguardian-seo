Our Story

Tech Guardian started with a simple frustration: watching people get told their devices were "unrepairable" when they absolutely weren't. Big box stores and corporate repair chains have one goal — sell you a new device. We have a different goal — fix what you have.

We invested in professional micro soldering equipment, trained extensively in board-level repair, and built a shop where "impossible" is just another Tuesday. When Apple says your MacBook needs a $1,200 logic board replacement, we fix the $2 chip that actually failed.

That's the Tech Guardian difference. We're technicians, not salespeople. We fix things.
