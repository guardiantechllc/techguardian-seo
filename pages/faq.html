Repairs & Pricing

How long do repairs take?

Most repairs are completed same-day! iPhone screens typically take 30-60 minutes. More complex repairs like MacBook logic boards or water damage may take 1-3 days. We'll always give you an estimated timeline upfront.

What parts do you use?

We use high-quality OEM-equivalent parts that meet or exceed original specifications. For certain repairs, we can source genuine OEM parts upon request (may affect pricing and availability).

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes! All repairs come with a 90-day warranty on parts and labor. If something goes wrong with our repair, bring it back and we'll make it right - no questions asked.

What if you can't fix it?

No fix, no fee. If we can't repair your device, you don't pay anything. Simple as that. We'll also give you honest advice about whether repair makes sense versus replacement.

How much does repair cost?

Pricing varies by device and repair type. Check our Specials page for current pricing. We're always transparent - you'll know the exact cost before we start any work.

Specific Devices

Can you fix water damaged devices?

Yes! We specialize in water damage recovery. The sooner you bring it in, the better the chances. Don't put it in rice - bring it to us! We use professional ultrasonic cleaning and board-level repair to maximize recovery chances.

Do you fix gaming consoles?

Absolutely! We repair PS5, Xbox Series X/S, Nintendo Switch, Steam Deck, and retro consoles. Common repairs include HDMI ports, disc drives, overheating issues, and controller drift.

Can you recover data from a dead phone?

In most cases, yes! We've recovered data from phones that other shops said were impossible. We use board-level techniques to access data even when the phone won't turn on. Bring it in for a free evaluation.

What MacBook repairs do you do?

We do board-level MacBook repairs including: logic board repair, liquid damage recovery, no power issues, charging problems, screen replacement, keyboard replacement, and more. We fix what Apple says is "unrepairable."

Payment & Policies

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, all major credit/debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Venmo. Payment is due when you pick up your repaired device.

Will repair void my warranty?

Third-party repair may affect your manufacturer warranty. However, if your device is already out of warranty or the damage isn't covered anyway, we're often a faster and more affordable option. We're happy to discuss your specific situation.

Is my data safe during repair?

Yes! We respect your privacy. We never access, copy, or look at your personal data unless specifically needed for data recovery services you've requested. We recommend backing up before any repair when possible.
