Repairs & Turnaround
What is board-level micro soldering? +
Board-level micro soldering is a specialized repair technique where we fix individual components on your device's circuit board under a microscope. Instead of replacing a $400 logic board, we fix the $2 chip that actually failed. This lets us repair devices that other shops say are "unrepairable" -- saving you hundreds of dollars.
How long do repairs take? +
Most standard repairs (screen replacements, HDMI ports, battery swaps) are completed same-day, often while you wait. Complex board-level repairs may take 24-48 hours depending on the diagnosis. We'll always give you an honest timeline upfront.
