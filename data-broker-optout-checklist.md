# Data Broker Opt-Out Checklist

Work through these one by one. Most take 24-72 hours to process. Some require email verification. Check back in 2-4 weeks to verify removal.

---

## BEFORE YOU START

- [ ] Create a throwaway email for opt-outs (so you don't get more spam)
- [ ] Google yourself: `"Your Full Name" + city` - screenshot what you find
- [ ] Check https://haveibeenpwned.com for breached accounts
- [ ] Have your info ready: full name, addresses (current + past), phone numbers, email addresses

## GOOGLE & SOCIAL MEDIA

- [ ] **Google Search Results** - https://support.google.com/websearch/troubleshooter/3111061
  - Request removal of personal info from Google results

- [ ] **Google Activity** - https://myactivity.google.com
  - Delete your activity history

- [ ] **Facebook** - Settings > Privacy > Off-Facebook Activity
  - Clear history, turn off tracking

- [ ] **Instagram** - Settings > Privacy > Activity Status
  - Limit data sharing

- [ ] **LinkedIn** - Settings > Visibility > Profile viewing options
  - Go private mode

- [ ] **Twitter/X** - Settings > Privacy > Discoverability
  - Turn off phone/email lookup

## FOLLOW UP (2-4 weeks later)

- [ ] Google yourself again - compare to original screenshots
- [ ] Re-check major sites (Spokeo, WhitePages, TruePeopleSearch)
- [ ] Re-submit any that didn't process
- [ ] Set calendar reminder to check again in 3 months (they re-add you)

---

## PRO TIPS

1. **Use a VPN** when doing opt-outs so they don't track your IP
2. **Screenshot everything** before and after
3. **Some sites re-add you** - check back every 3-6 months
4. **Email templates** - if they ask why, just say "personal privacy concerns"
5. **CCPA/GDPR requests** work even if you're not in CA/EU - companies often honor them anyway. Use language like: "Under CCPA, I request deletion of my personal information"
6. **Freeze your credit** at all 3 bureaus to stop new accounts:
   - Equifax: https://www.equifax.com/personal/credit-report-services/credit-freeze/
   - Experian: https://www.experian.com/freeze/
   - TransUnion: https://www.transunion.com/credit-freeze

```
Full Name: [YOUR NAME]
Addresses: [CURRENT AND PAST ADDRESSES]

Please confirm removal within 45 days as required by law.

Thank you,
[YOUR NAME]
```
